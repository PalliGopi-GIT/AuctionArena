# Cloudflare transition plan

The selected target is Workers/Hono, React/Vite, server-verified Clerk, and a SQLite-backed Durable Object per room with WebSocket hibernation and alarms. R2 is optional and inactive. The root master records the Cloudflare restoration and remaining gaps. The owner authorized package A (Phase 2 local hardening) on 13 November 2026; its implementation and limits are in [WP2-LOCAL-BOUNDS](tasks/WP2-LOCAL-BOUNDS.md). The owner subsequently authorized the local sequence B–F; its current state is below.

After audit review, implement in order: A. bounded heartbeat writes, history, receipts, replay and paging in the local runtime; B. storage ports and SQLite parity tests; C. Workers/Hono entry, Clerk and room-code routing under local Wrangler/Miniflare; D. room Durable Object, hibernation, alarms, recovery and settlement; E. cross-room index, archive, deletion and export; F. measured multi-room 100/500/1,000-concurrent-user tests.

Packages B–F are implemented in the uncommitted working tree. Packages B–E passed their local verification. Package E uses a single global SQLite-backed directory object as an eventually consistent projection fed by a durable room outbox, while room-local SQLite remains authoritative; its scope and limits are in [WP-E-CROSS-ROOM-DIRECTORY](tasks/WP-E-CROSS-ROOM-DIRECTORY.md). Package F measured the local runtime at 100, 500 and 1,000 users: 100 passed the local criteria, while 500 and 1,000 failed. Its scope, results and limits are in [WP-F-MEASURED-LOAD](tasks/WP-F-MEASURED-LOAD.md). These are local measurements, not Cloudflare or production capacity claims.

Package G then ran the real Worker and room Durable Object in local `wrangler dev`/workerd with SQLite state, hibernating WebSockets and alarms. All four measured levels (100/250/500/1,000 users) passed with zero HTTP, socket or ping errors, every expected broadcast delivered and every room settled; settlement integrity, lifecycle and full-restart persistence also passed. The scope, results and remaining local-runtime limits are in [WP-G-WORKERS-RUNTIME](tasks/WP-G-WORKERS-RUNTIME.md). After Package G, the remaining profile-update integration was completed: the Worker accepts `PATCH /api/me`, updates Clerk, fans the display name to existing rooms and strips client-supplied internal operation headers. The post-integration regression is 264 core tests, 76 local tests, a successful build, 34/34 browser cases and 4/4 accessibility cases.

Staging is deployed and audited. `env.staging` targets `auctionarena-staging`, declares `ROOM`, `DIRECTORY`, `CLERK_ISSUER` and `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` explicitly (Wrangler does not inherit object bindings or vars into environments) while inheriting the two SQLite migrations from the top level. Wrangler 4.162.0 deployed version `cc191a81-279a-4d85-bf4e-28145a002d16` at 100% to https://auctionarena-staging.palligopi1924.workers.dev with 8 bindings and no R2/D1/KV/Queue/Pages resource; production `auctionarena` does not exist. Live checks covered health/config, CORS allow and deny, 401s, the served bundle, Clerk's allowed origin, room create/refresh/reconnect and a two-user bid. Two client defects were fixed: room listings without `members` crashed the room list into a blank page, and the socket URL omitted the required `?room=`. The audit, costs, rollback and result are in [STAGING-DEPLOYMENT-PLAN](../reports/STAGING-DEPLOYMENT-PLAN.md), with captured output in [evidence/STAGING-DEPLOYMENT](../reports/evidence/STAGING-DEPLOYMENT/README.md).

The per-room object is the proposed single writer. It does not by itself solve authorization, cross-room queries, exports, global user deletion or exactly-once settlement. Idempotency and settlement must be transactional and proven with restart, retry and recovery tests. idFromName routing must not make non-existent rooms publicly discoverable or create rooms on unauthorized access. Retain the local runtime until a replacement passes parity tests.

No deployment, account or setting creation, credit card, R2, production migrations or cost without explicit owner approval; budget $0 out of pocket. Preserve existing evidence, fixtures, scripts, migrations and infrastructure. Historical records are in ../archive/aws-v2/; physical deletion is a separate later approval after Cloudflare tests pass.
